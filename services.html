Our services

Understanding the whole. Engineering the parts. Delivering the result.

EF Cactus works across business, commercial, financial, legal, regulatory, operational and technological disciplines. Our services reflect the interconnected nature of real organisational problems.

One integrated proposition

Organisations rarely encounter problems that fit neatly within a single department or professional discipline. A product may require commercial design, financial modelling, contractual documentation, regulatory analysis, operational procedures and technology implementation.

These are not necessarily separate assignments. They are often different dimensions of the same problem.

EF Cactus can address individual requirements or work across several areas as part of an integrated engagement.

01

Business, Commercial and Financial Architecture

Designing the commercial, financial and contractual foundations on which products, services and business activities operate.

Business and Commercial Architecture

Analysis and design of business models, commercial structures, propositions, operating relationships and the arrangements required to make them viable and sustainable.

Financial Product Architecture

Development of financial products and services from their underlying commercial rationale through product structure, parameters, rules, conditions, documentation and implementation.

This includes product lifecycle design, product frameworks, customer propositions, operational requirements and the contractual mechanisms through which products function.

Contractual and Regulatory Design

Analysis and development of contractual structures, terms of business, agreements, regulatory requirements and the relationships between legal obligations and practical business operations.

02

Operational and Technology Engineering

Translating business requirements and regulatory obligations into workable operating structures, processes and systems.

Operational Engineering and Governance

Design and improvement of operating models, procedures, internal controls, organisational responsibilities, governance arrangements and decision-making structures.

Technology and Operational Risk

Examination of technology choices, system dependencies, operational exposure, information flows and control arrangements. Focus on proportionality, resilience, independence and the practical consequences of architectural decisions.

03

Project, Programme and Transformation Management

Taking complex initiatives from definition and design through controlled implementation and operational delivery.

Project and Programme Management

Project definition, planning, work breakdown, resource and dependency management, governance, delivery control and coordination of complex initiatives.

Transformation and Implementation

Design and delivery of organisational, operational, regulatory and technological change. Ensuring that proposed solutions become functioning business arrangements rather than remaining plans or documents.

04

Coaching, Mentoring and Capability Development

Developing the people, knowledge and organisational capabilities needed to sustain effective performance.

Management and Professional Coaching

Practical support for managers and professionals dealing with complex responsibilities, organisational challenges and demanding delivery environments.

Project and Programme Coaching

Developing project leadership, structured problem-solving, planning, governance and delivery capabilities.

Operational Development and Knowledge Transfer

Strengthening internal capabilities through structured knowledge transfer, practical training, procedure development and the establishment of repeatable working methods.
