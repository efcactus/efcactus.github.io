Featured perspective

Elimination Before Mitigation

Why spend resources managing the consequences of complexity that should never have been introduced?

Organisations often respond to risk by adding controls, procedures and technical safeguards around existing arrangements. But an important question is frequently overlooked: was the original arrangement necessary?

Effective risk management should begin by examining the underlying business requirement and challenging unnecessary complexity before attempting to mitigate its consequences.
