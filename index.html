OUR PROPOSITION

Connecting disciplines. Engineering workable solutions.

Complex business challenges rarely fit neatly within the boundaries of a single department or professional discipline. Commercial decisions have contractual consequences. Regulatory obligations affect products and operations. Technology choices influence governance, resilience and risk.

EF Cactus brings these considerations together. We examine the substance of the problem, understand how its different elements interact and develop solutions that are coherent, proportionate and workable.

Our involvement extends beyond analysis and recommendations. We can design the arrangements, manage the work and support implementation through to practical operation.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Elimination Before Mitigation

Eliminate unnecessary complexity before spending money managing its consequences.

Organisations often invest considerable resources in controlling the risks created by decisions that were never properly questioned.

We believe in examining necessity before complexity, and eliminating unnecessary exposure before introducing layers of mitigation and control.

Sound architecture begins with asking whether something needs to exist at all — not simply how to manage the consequences of its existence.
